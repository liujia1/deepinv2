## Control - Enhanced Bicycle Model

$$
\begin{array}{l} \dot {s} = \frac {v _ {x} \cos (\mu) - v _ {y} \sin (\mu)}{1 - n \kappa (s)} \\ \dot {n} = v _ {x} \sin (\mu) + v _ {y} \cos (\mu) \\ \dot {\mu} = \dot {\phi} - \kappa (s) \dot {s} \\ \dot {v} _ {x} = \frac {1}{m} (F _ {x _ {r}} - F _ {d} - F _ {y _ {f}} \sin (\delta) + F _ {x _ {f}} \cos (\delta) - F _ {b _ {x}} + m v _ {y} \dot {\phi}) \\ \dot {v} _ {y} = \frac {1}{m} (F _ {y _ {r}} + F _ {y _ {f}} \cos (\delta) + F _ {x _ {f}} \sin (\delta) - F _ {b _ {y}} - m v _ {x} \dot {\phi}) \\ \ddot {\phi} = \frac {1}{I _ {z}} (l _ {f} (F _ {y _ {f}} \cos (\delta) + F _ {x _ {f}} \sin (\delta)) - l _ {r} F _ {y _ {r}}) \\ \dot {\delta} = \Delta \delta \\ \dot {T} = \Delta T \\ \dot {B} = \Delta B \end{array}
$$
